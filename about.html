About GameZone

Welcome to GameZone!

GameZone is a collection of 10 free HTML5 games that you can play directly in your browser. No downloads, no installations, no accounts required — just click and play!

Our Games

We offer a variety of classic and modern games including:

  • 🐍 Snake - Classic arcade fun
  • 🧱 Tetris - Block-stacking puzzle
  • 🐦 Flappy Bird - Tap-to-fly challenge
  • 🃏 Memory Cards - Brain training
  • ❌ Tic Tac Toe - Strategy vs AI
  • 🔢 2048 - Number puzzle
  • 💥 Breakout - Brick-breaking action
  • 🔨 Whack-a-Mole - Reflexes test
  • 🧠 Quiz Game - Trivia challenge
  • 📝 Word Scramble - Word puzzle

Technology

All games are built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. They use HTML5 Canvas for rendering and work on all modern browsers and mobile devices.
