Last Updated: June 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better browsing experience, and supply information to the website owners.

Cookies allow a website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, font size, and other display preferences. This means you don't have to re-enter this information each time you visit.

2. How We Use Cookies

Game Zone uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Operations: To ensure our Site functions properly and provides the gaming experience you expect
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors interact with our Site and games, helping us improve our platform
  • Advertising: To deliver relevant advertisements and measure advertising campaign effectiveness
  • Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences for a better experience

5. Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to collect information about how visitors use our Site. This helps us improve our Site and provide a better gaming experience. The information collected is aggregated and anonymous.

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your use of our Site, including your IP address (which is anonymized before storage). This information is transmitted to and stored by Google. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics cookies include:

  • _ga: Used to distinguish unique users (expires after 2 years)
  • _ga_*: Used to persist session state (expires after 2 years)
  • _gid: Used to distinguish users (expires after 24 hours)
  • _gat: Used to throttle request rate (expires after 1 minute)

In addition to cookies, we use local storage (HTML5 Web Storage) to store game data on your device, including:

Local storage data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

9. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in various ways. Please keep in mind that removing or blocking cookies may negatively impact your gaming experience and parts of the Site may no longer be fully accessible.

Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies through their settings. You can typically find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data

Opt-Out Links:

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Google Ads: Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative: NAI Consumer Opt-out

10. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our Site, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your cookies and revisiting the Site.

Essential cookies will always be used as they are necessary for the proper functioning of the Site. By continuing to use our Site after being presented with the cookie notice, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.
